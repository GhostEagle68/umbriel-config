**A redesigned app.** A darker charcoal look, a Home screen, and every page rebuilt around plain rows.
- **Home.** The app opens on a drawing of your desktop: your monitors where they sit, your wallpaper, and windows tiled with your gap, border, radius and colors. Below it: your monitors, layout, config files and umbriel, plus anything waiting for you.
- **Sidebar.** Search at the top, an icon on every row, and collapsible groups. Keybinds now sit under Input, Animations and Shaders under Look, and rules under a new Windows group.
- **Notices.** Short messages appear in the corner and fade on their own; only failures that need you open a dialog. Save shows up in the top bar only when there's something to save.
- **Shaders.** Assignments and Library now have their own tabs, with a thumbnail for every shader. A picker plays shaders live as you hover. Border, window, screen and cursor effects can be assigned from the Assignments tab too.
- **New settings stay marked.** A setting umbriel adds keeps its "New" badge, across restarts, until you open its page. Or clear them all from Home.
- **File viewer.** Opening a config file from a setting shows it with colors and line numbers, scrolled to the line that sets that setting. It can copy the path or open the file in your editor.
