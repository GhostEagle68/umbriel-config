umbriel-config — install

  1. Open a terminal here.
  2. Run:  ./install.sh
  3. Start "Umbriel Config" from your application menu, or run:
     umbriel-config

install.sh copies the app into ~/.local with no root, nothing outside your
home directory. For a system-wide install instead:

  sudo PREFIX=/usr/local ./install.sh

Updating
  Installed this way, the app updates itself: Settings -> Updates ->
  Install update. Pick the Stable or Pre-release channel there too.

Uninstalling
  rm ~/.local/bin/umbriel-config
  rm ~/.local/share/applications/umbriel-config.desktop
  rm ~/.local/share/icons/hicolor/scalable/apps/umbriel-config.svg
  rm -r ~/.local/share/licenses/umbriel-config

  Your umbriel config files and the backups the app made are your own
  data; nothing here touches them.
